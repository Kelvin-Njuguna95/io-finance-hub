Our operating thesis

The best AI teams want an operation, not a platform.

When we started Impact in 2021, most data partners on the market were built around one idea. Put a labelling tool in front of a crowd, collect consensus, and call that quality. That approach works until you try to ship a model. Real quality comes from people who are trained on your task, measured against your schema, and held to the same delivery discipline your own product team runs on.

So we built the opposite of a crowdsourced marketplace. Every client gets a named team and a single delivery lead. Inter annotator agreement is tracked like a production metric. Quality reviews sit inside the workflow rather than bolted on at the end. Our information security program is ISO 27001 certified because the data you send us is the data your model runs on.

We are a managed data operations partner. Annotation, validation, and AI model training services are delivered from a single place, by a single team, for as long as you need them.

How we operate

Four principles we do not negotiate away.

01 / Managed

Named teams. One delivery lead. No anonymous queues.

Every engagement is staffed with a dedicated team and a single accountable lead. When something needs attention, you know exactly who is handling it and how to reach them.

02 / Measured

Quality is a first class metric.

Inter annotator agreement, multi tier review, and written accuracy thresholds sit inside every statement of work. Quality reports go out every week of delivery, for the lifetime of the engagement.

03 / Secure

Enterprise grade information security, by default.

ISO 27001 certified information security management system. Access controlled environments. NDAs on day one. Sensitive data is handled to the same standard your internal teams already apply.

04 / Equitable

Fair, full time work. No gig labour.

Above market wages. Documented progression paths. A workforce that is 60 percent women. An AI data supply chain that a responsible buyer is comfortable naming in public.
